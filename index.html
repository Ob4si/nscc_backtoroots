What the theme means

Back to the Roots celebrates Nigerian heritage through village life, cultural traditions and the vibrancy of 1960s highlife. Through nostalgic imagery and storytelling we honor our ancestors, celebrate our history and reconnect with our roots.

Post-independence

Nigeria in the 1960s: a new flag, new music, a country finding its voice.

Reconnecting

Returning to something many of us have lost touch with, abroad or at home.

Village practice

The cultural and the everyday: markets, compounds, ceremonies, chores.

Roots travel

Roots take hold wherever we are in the diaspora, in the village and in the city.
